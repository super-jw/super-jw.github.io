About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Trustworthy Machine Learning and Reasoning (TMLR) group in the Department of Computer Science, Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Bo Han. Previously, I received both my M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees from Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, particularly improving the safety, robustness, and reliability of foundation models (LLMs and VLMs). My current interests include adversarial attacks, test-time adaptation, GUI agents, and agent memory.

My long-term goal is to build adaptive AI systems that remain dependable as they evolve in complex environments. I am always open to discussions and possible collaborations. Please feel free to email me.
