I am a Ph.D. student at Trustworthy Machine Learning and Reasoning (TMLR) group in the Department of Computer Science, Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU), advised by Prof. Bo Han. Previously, I received both of my MPhil Eng. degree and Bachelor Eng. degree from Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research interests focus on trustworthy machine learning, particularly in improving the safety, robustness, and reliability of foundation models (LLMs, VLMs), with specific research topics such as adversarial attacks, test-time adaptation, GUI agents, and agent memory. My long-term goal is to build adaptive AI systems that remain dependable as they evolve in complex environments.

I am always open to discussions and possible collaborations. Please feel free to email me if you would like to chat.
